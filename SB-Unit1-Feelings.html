1F · Ex 3Match the questions with the paragraphs

In which paragraph (A–E) does the writer tell us …? Choose 'none' if there is no matching paragraph.

A life without pain

A All children hurt themselves from time to time. But when thirteen-year-old Ashlyn Blocker was younger, she had more accidents and injuries than her friends. For example, she once put her hands on a very hot engine and got a serious burn. She only knew about it when she looked at her skin. Ashlyn simply did not know when she injured herself.

B When Ashlyn was a baby, her parents knew she was different: she didn't cry. When she was eight months old, they noticed some blood in her eye and took her to a doctor. The doctor was shocked and confused: there was a serious cut, but the baby wasn't upset. Doctors realised Ashlyn had a very unusual medical condition: she couldn't feel any pain.

C This condition is very rare: only about a hundred people a year in the USA are born with it, and many of them die because of it. Pain is a natural warning: when you're ill or injured, your body hurts. People who can't feel pain often die young because when they break a bone or have a heart problem, they just don't realise.

D The first few years of Ashlyn's life were very difficult. She often tripped and injured herself. At school, a teacher watched Ashlyn all the time to keep her safe.

E When she was five, Ashlyn's story appeared in newspapers. She had invitations to appear on TV shows and became well known. Scientists studied her condition and found it was a genetic disorder: pain signals do not reach her brain. Unfortunately, there is no cure.
